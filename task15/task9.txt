# Task 9 – Deploy Strapi on ECS using Fargate Spot

## 📝 Objective

To optimize infrastructure cost by switching from **Fargate (on-demand)** to **Fargate Spot**, while keeping the ECS deployment structure mostly the same.

---

## ⚙️ What We Did

1. **Updated the ECS Service definition** in Terraform:
   - Set `capacity_provider_strategy` with `FARGATE_SPOT`.
   - Gave full weight to `FARGATE_SPOT` and removed `FARGATE` provider.
2. **Retained all previous configuration**:
   - ALB for traffic routing.
   - CloudWatch Logs for observability.
   - ECR for container images.
   - IAM roles and security groups.
3. **Tested availability** of the Strapi app through the ALB endpoint after deployment.

---

## 🧠 Learnings

- Understood how **Fargate Spot** works: offers the same serverless container deployment experience at up to 70% reduced cost.
- Learned to define capacity provider strategy in ECS services via Terraform.
- Observed the limitations of Fargate Spot (e.g., **may be interrupted**, no guaranteed availability).
- Realized that Fargate Spot is a good fit for dev/test or non-critical workloads.

---

## 🚧 Challenges Faced

- Initially forgot to associate the `FARGATE_SPOT` capacity provider with the ECS cluster (fixed via Terraform).
- Deployment failed until we removed the default `launch_type = "FARGATE"` setting.
- Had to monitor ECS logs to ensure service stability under Spot conditions.

---

## ✅ Outcome

- Strapi successfully deployed on **Fargate Spot**, reducing compute cost.
- Retained all previous features: ALB access, monitoring, logging.
- Infrastructure is now more cost-efficient without sacrificing performance for dev use.
